# Diary

What happened on one day and belongs to nothing else: an outing, a dinner, a first time, a person met, a decision about life and its reasons.

- **One file for each day:** `Life/Diary/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, named after the day it happened, not the day it was written.
- **Append,** never rewrite. Each entry is a short paragraph or a few lines.
- **The diary is written in the person's own voice,** in the first person. It is the only exception to impersonal notes.
- **Link what an entry mentions:** the person, the case, the object.

**Not here:**
- A fact that stays true, such as a new allergy or a new address → its folder. The diary can add one line that links to it.
- A plan for a day still to come → the calendar.
- A matter with steps → `Life/Cases/`.
