# Finances

Money that stays: accounts, cards, loans, investment and pension plans, subscriptions, the phone line, health insurance, a will.

- **One note for each account or contract,** such as `Example Bank Joint Account.md` or `Netflix.md`. Many small subscriptions can share one note, `Subscriptions.md`, as a table.
- **What each note holds:** the provider, who holds it, the last digits of the account or the policy number, the cost and how often, the renewal date, and how to cancel.
- **The contract itself** sits beside its note, as a small file.
- **Never write a full card number, a PIN or a password** in a note. A full card number, a card PIN or a code goes in a `.secrets` file beside the note. An online-banking login goes in the password manager.
- **A goal, such as saving for a home,** goes in the note of its account, or in `Goals.md`.

**Not here:**
- A contract of a thing or a home, such as car insurance or the rent → that thing's folder in `Life/Objects/`.
- A matter with steps that ends, such as this year's tax return, a refund or a dispute → `Life/Cases/`.
- A single payment reminder → the task app.
- How the person decides money matters, such as an investing method → `Methods/`.

**Closing:** When a contract or account ends, write the end date in its note. Never move or delete the note.
