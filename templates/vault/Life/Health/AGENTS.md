# Health

Body and mind: allergies, diet, sleep, measurements, conditions, test results, goals, and what stays after a surgery or a treatment.

- **One note for each topic,** such as `Sleep.md`, `Measurements.md` or `Thyroid.md`. Add to the note of a topic before you create a new one.
- **`Allergies and Diet.md`** comes with the vault. Keep its `summary` line true: it says the allergies and the diet in one line.
- **A test result or a medical report** sits beside the note of its topic, as a small file.
- **Measurements that change,** such as weight, are dated lines, newest first.
- **A lasting condition** gets its own note: what it is, since when, treatment, and what to avoid.

**Not here:**
- The process of a surgery or a treatment: appointments, papers, recovery → `Life/Cases/`. When the case closes, add what stays to the note of its topic here.
- A training plan or a routine to follow → `Methods/`.
- A doctor as a person → `Life/People/`. Their phone → the contacts app.
- Health insurance → `Life/Finances/`.
